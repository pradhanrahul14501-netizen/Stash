# Stash paid beta plan

## Positioning

Stash is a private clipboard and media library for people who regularly copy useful videos, files, links, and text and need to retrieve them later.
Unlike Paste's cross-device clipboard history, Stash centers an encrypted local library and retrieval through Ava.
Unlike Raycast's clipboard history, Stash is designed to keep copied media and files searchable alongside text, with a local-first workflow.

## Initial persona and painful job

**Persona:** Mac-based video editors and creator-researchers who collect clips, source links, and notes while researching several projects each week.

**Painful job:** Find a specific clip or source they copied earlier without replaying the browsing session or searching browser history.

## Interview questions for 10–15 target users

1. Tell me about the last time you needed to find a clip, link, or note you copied earlier.
2. What were you trying to finish when you needed it?
3. Where did you look first, and what did you try next?
4. How long did retrieval take, and what happened if you could not find it?
5. What kinds of copied media or notes do you need to retrieve most often?
6. How do you currently separate sources for different projects?
7. What information would you trust a clipboard library to store locally?
8. What would make you refuse to use a tool that sees clipboard content?
9. How often would you expect to retrieve old copies in a normal week?
10. What would you need to see in a 10-minute trial before paying for it?

## Paid beta and metrics

- Invite 10–15 interview participants to a two-week paid beta with a clear refund path and direct support.
- **Activation:** percentage of invited users who save 10 items and retrieve one within 24 hours.
- **Weekly retrievals:** median successful retrieval actions per active user per week; track searches that open/copy a result.
- **Four-week retention:** percentage of activated users who retrieve at least one item in week four.
- **Conversion:** percentage of beta users who choose a paid plan after the beta, measured only after price and plan are shown.
- **Launch gate:** a clean macOS install must install locked dependencies, start Stash, unlock the encrypted library, save a copy, find it, open it, and survive restart without data loss.
